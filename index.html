Robot & practice settings

How the trainer models the game

Alliance score = SUPPRESSION balls × (1 + climb bonuses) + EXTINGUISHER + Coopertition
  • 7 × 7 m field. Structure positions, brace, fire shields and ports are measured from the official FG26_F field drawing. Red drives from the left wall, blue from the right.
  • 2:30 match, all driver controlled, 3 robots per alliance, no defense.
  • Your robot is modelled on your build: a two-layer square extrusion frame with an X-drive (HD Hex motors, UltraPlanetary 4:1 + 3:1, omni wheels at 45°), a chain roller pickup with a folding collector, a banded belt feeder, a six-wheel thrower, a storage box for about 38 balls and a two-wheel pole climber. The other robots are the REV DUO starter bot with tank drive.
  • X-drive maths: Stick input goes through the usual FTC field-centric mix (rotate by the IMU heading, then front-left = y + x + turn, and so on, divided by the biggest sum). Straight or sideways is about 1.4 times faster than driving diagonally, and turning while driving slows you down, the same as the real robot.
  • Climb bonus per robot: Contact +5%, Zone 1 +10%, Zone 2 +20%, Zone 3 +30%. Zones are thirds of the brace, Zone 1 at the low end by the fire shield. Coopertition: 4, 5 or 6 robots in Zone 3 adds 10, 25 or 40.
  • The suppression unit edge is 165 cm high. The thrower launches at 55° from 45 cm, so balls score when your bumper is roughly 1.2 to 2.9 m from the front edge and you face it square.
  • Balls ejected into your FIRE SHIELD PORT go to your human player, who throws them into the shared EXTINGUISHER (about 3 in 4 go in).

Estimates, not from the manual

  • Ball size (12 cm), where balls start, balls coming back out under the extinguisher, human player speed and accuracy.
  • The brace blocks robots only on its lowest 30%. You can attach only in the Zone 1 part, facing up the brace within 25°.
  • Carrying a partner (+25) is not modeled. Robot size is 45 cm square.
