Robot & practice settings

How the trainer models the game

Alliance score = (SUPPRESSION UNIT balls × (1 + climb increments)) + partner climbs + EXTINGUISHER + Coopertition
  • The field is built from the official FG26_F CAD: every panel of the suppression units, extinguisher and fire shields is in the 3D view and in the ball physics. The field is 70 cm above the floor and the guardrail is 20 cm above the carpet. You stand on the floor in your alliance station.
  • 500 WILDFIRE (100 mm foam, 62 g) start inside the extinguisher and come out from underneath it after "go". Nothing is preloaded (G06). A ball counts when it is CONTAINED at the end, 5 s after 0:00 when everything has come to rest (G21).
  • Suppression unit: the front wall is 150.6 cm high, and the canopy slopes down from 200 cm to a lip 165 cm high and 40 cm out. A steep shot can hit the underside of the canopy and drop into the opening, where a ramp rolls it into the front column. 1 point per ball, for your alliance.
  • Human players: robots push balls into their fire shield PORT (low on the slanted face). Your human player takes each one out and throws it into the extinguisher (1 point for both alliances), or sends it down the CHUTE to your robot. Set how many seconds each ball takes (default 4) and how many throws go in (default 70%).
  • Brace: a 6.4 m pipe from 24 cm (by the fire shield) to 197 cm (the extinguisher's top corner), split into three 2.08 m zones. Climb multiplier per robot: Contact +0.05, Zone 1 +0.10, Zone 2 +0.20, Zone 3 +0.30. You must start the climb in Zone 1, fully off the carpet (G15). The brace sags up to 10 cm under load. Coopertition: 4, 5 or 6 robots in Zone 3 adds 10, 25 or 40 for everyone.
  • Your robot: two-layer square extrusion frame, X-drive (HD Hex, UltraPlanetary 4:1 + 3:1, omni wheels at 45° in the corners), chain roller pickup with a folding collector, banded feeder, six-wheel thrower, storage for about 38 balls and a two-wheel pole climber. Robot centric by default (forward on the stick is always the robot's front). Triangle switches to field centric.
  • IMU: heading hold and field centric read a simulated Control Hub IMU at 100 Hz with a little noise and a slowly wandering zero, and the motors aren't perfectly matched. So when you drive straight the robot pulls slightly and the IMU makes small corrections, like your real robot. Share resets the IMU.
  • Physics: every wheel is a DC motor model (HD Hex: 6000 rpm free, 0.105 N·m stall) pushing the robot and the balls it carries. Robots push balls and get slowed down by piles.

Estimates, not in the manual or CAD

  • How fast the extinguisher lets the 500 balls out (default: over 20 s), how bouncy the foam balls are, and how far they roll on the carpet.
  • Where the human player stands, and how they throw. Robots' shots into the extinguisher don't count (only human players score there).
  • How your robot hangs on the brace (its climber grips the pipe about 42 cm above its base). Carrying a partner (+25) isn't modelled.
